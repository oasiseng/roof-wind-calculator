ASCE 7-22 Roof Components & Cladding Wind Pressure Calculator

Gable and hip roofs, 0° to 45° · h ≤ 60 ft · Figs. 30.3-2A to 30.3-2G · v2.0

Scope: enclosed, partially open and partially enclosed buildings with mean roof height h ≤ 60 ft, gable or hip roofs from flat to 45° (ASCE 7-22 Chapter 30, Part 1). Roofs at 7° or less use Fig. 30.3-2A whatever their shape. Not covered: roof overhangs (Sec. 30.7), parapets, monoslope roofs steeper than 3°, stepped, sawtooth or multispan roofs, open buildings, rooftop equipment, and tornado loads (Chapter 32).

How to Use

Enter the wind speed, the building, the roof and the effective wind area of the component you are sizing. Results update as you type: design pressure for every roof zone at strength level and at ASD (0.6 W), a zone drawing in proportion to your plan, and a table across common effective wind areas.

Pressures follow Eq. 30.3-1, p = qh Kd [(GCp) − (GCpi)], with Kd = 0.85 and the 16 psf minimum of Sec. 30.2.2. Kh uses z = 15 ft for any height below 15 ft, in every exposure (Table 26.10-1).
